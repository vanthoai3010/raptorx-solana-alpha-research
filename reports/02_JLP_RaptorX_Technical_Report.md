# Technical Research Report: Jupiter Perps (JLP)

## 1. What It Is

**Jupiter Perps LP (JLP)** is the native liquidity provider token of Jupiter Perpetuals Exchange, one of the most prominent decentralized perpetual futures trading platforms built on the Solana blockchain. The token serves as the backbone of Jupiter's perps trading engine, allowing liquidity providers to deposit assets into the protocol's pool and earn yield from trading fees and funding rates generated by the platform's perpetual futures market.

The project operates under the ticker **JLP** with the contract address `27G8MtK7VtTcCHkpASjSDdkWWYfoqT6ggEuKidVJidD4` on the Solana network. Jupiter Perps LP tokens are categorized within the Decentralized Finance (DeFi), LP Tokens, and Solana Ecosystem sectors according to CoinGecko's classification system.

JLP functions as a multi-asset liquidity pool token that provides exposure to a basket of underlying assets used to facilitate perpetual trading on Jupiter's platform. Rather than being a traditional speculative token, JLP represents a yield-bearing position that captures value from the protocol's trading activity. The token's value is directly tied to the performance of the underlying pool assets, which include major cryptocurrencies such as SOL, BTC, ETH, and various stablecoins.

The project's homepage is accessible at [jup.ag](https://jup.ag/), with additional resources including a Discord community ([discord.com/invite/jup](https://discord.com/invite/jup)), blog at [station.jup.ag](https://station.jup.ag/blog/), and official Twitter account [@JupiterExchange](https://twitter.com/JupiterExchange). The project also maintains a Telegram channel under the handle "jup_marketing."

Jupiter Perps LP is listed on CoinGecko as "Jupiter Perpetuals Liquidity Provider Token" and is part of a broader ecosystem that includes Jupiter Exchange's suite of DeFi products, including Jupiter Lend, Jupiter Swap, and the recently launched GUM perps engine on the GumChain network.
